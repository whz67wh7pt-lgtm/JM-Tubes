About J & M Tubes

A family-run tube specialist in Wolverhampton.

J & M Tubes Ltd has been established since September 1982, supplying and cutting tube for customers who value accuracy, quick turnaround and a reliable finished product.

Our experienced team works with a broad range of metals and tube specifications. We can source material through our supplier network or process customer-supplied material on a free-issue basis.

From straightforward cut lengths to mitre cutting, deburring, cleaning, bundling and palletising, the aim is simple: make the process easy and deliver work to a consistently high standard.

What we do

Tube processing from supply to finished component.

Flexible tube cutting and finishing services for one-off, repeat and production requirements.

Tube Supply & Cutting

Tube sizes from 3/8" to 3" diameter, cut from 6mm to 3200mm with tolerances down to ±0.5mm.

Mitre Cutting

Accurate angled cuts produced to suit your component and fabrication requirements.

Vibro Deburring

Deburring to remove sharp edges and improve the finish of cut components before onward use.

Tube Cleaning

Cleaning services are available to help prepare components for the next stage of production or finishing.

Free-Issue Cutting

Supply your own material and we can carry out the same cutting, deburring, cleaning and finishing services.

Bundling & Palletising

Components can be bundled and palletised to your specification, with transport arrangements available where required.
